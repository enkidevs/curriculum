# Separate Criterion Scores

---
## Content

**Each eval criterion passes or fails on its own, so a failure on one stays visible however well the others score.**

This order was delivered 45 days ago, past the 30-day window. Here's the refund reply, scored on three criteria:

```plain-text
"So sorry! You can return it within 30 days
of delivery (Returns, section 1)."
  correct decision: FAIL
  cites the policy: PASS
  friendly tone:    PASS
```

Averaged into one score, the two passes lift this reply to 67%, and the wrong decision disappears into a middling number.

Scored separately, the decision's FAIL stays in view, and the reply fails, because the decision is what the customer acts on.

> ‼️ A common mistake is averaging criteria into one score. Keep required criteria, like the decision, as their own pass or fail, so a high score elsewhere can't hide a zero.
