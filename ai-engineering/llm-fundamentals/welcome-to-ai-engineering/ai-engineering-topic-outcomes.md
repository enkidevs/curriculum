# What You'll Learn

---
## Content

**AI engineering is the software engineering discipline focused on designing, testing, and deploying *reliable AI systems in production*.**

You'll start with large language models (LLMs), the kind of AI behind ChatGPT and Claude: how they work, and how to use one from your own code.

Soon you'll be able to:

- call a model from Python and get back data you can check
- let a model call your own functions safely
- estimate an AI feature's cost and speed it up

Then you'll build features that answer questions using your own documents, and agents that carry out tasks using your tools.

> 💡 By the end, you'll be able to take an AI feature from a working demo to something that keeps working with real users.
