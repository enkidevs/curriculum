# Course Overview

---
## Content

Here's what you'll learn next, in this order:

- **How AI assistants work:** what the assistant can and can't see when it answers you
- **Task briefs:** how to write a request the assistant can act on
- **Context and plans:** what to share with the assistant, and when to ask for a plan first
- **Review:** how to check what it gives you, and when to stop asking for changes

**After that, the course moves on to the work you already do: coding, debugging, documentation, and team communication.**

Each one uses things you already work with, like tickets, diffs, tests, logs, and docs.
