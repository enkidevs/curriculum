# Human-Owned Communication

---
## Content

Assistants can help with most of your writing at work. A few messages, though, carry your own judgment of a person or a situation, and the person receiving them is owed that judgment from you.

These messages stay yours to write:

- feedback about a person's work
- judgments that name an individual
- employment or disciplinary messages
- the final statement accepting responsibility for an incident

An assistant can still help around them. After an incident, it can organize the verified facts, and the accountable person writes the apology and owns it. If you can't defend a statement to the person receiving it, don't send it.

Here's the team norm:

```plain-text
Team norm: sensitive personal judgments and final accountability stay
human-written and human-owned.
```

**What decides it is who's responsible for the message, however accurate or kind a draft sounds.**

> 💡 Use an assistant for the parts you can check, like gathering facts. Write the judgment yourself.
