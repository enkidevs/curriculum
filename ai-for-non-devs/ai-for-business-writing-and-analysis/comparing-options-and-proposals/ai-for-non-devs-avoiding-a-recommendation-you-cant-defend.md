# A Recommendation You Can Defend

---
## Content

AI can suggest Vendor B and give you a confident paragraph explaining why.

**That suggestion becomes your recommendation only after you check and accept its reasoning.**

A short decision record shows that reasoning:

```plain-text
Choice: Vendor B
Why: Meets every must-have and leads on our weighted priorities
Evidence: Proposal pages and checked calculations
Tradeoffs accepted: Higher setup cost, shorter support hours
Still unknown: Weekend response time
Would change the choice: No confirmed weekend coverage
```

Settle any unknown that could change the choice, like the weekend response time. Then check that the evidence backs each reason and that the people affected accept the tradeoffs.

> 💡 A defensible recommendation shows why this option wins and what could make it lose.
