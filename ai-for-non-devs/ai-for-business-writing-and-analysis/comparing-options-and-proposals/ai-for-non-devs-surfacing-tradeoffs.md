# Surfacing Tradeoffs

---
## Content

A feature list says what each checkout system includes. It does not show what choosing one means.

Say the cheapest proposal only has weekday support. The lower price and the weekend risk come as a pair.

**A better request asks what you gain, what you give up, and when it matters:**

```plain-text
For each viable option, state what we gain, what we give up, and when that tradeoff would matter. Name any unanswered risk. Use only information in the proposals and cite its location.
```

This might show that the lower price means fixing a Saturday breakdown with no vendor help. That doesn't make the cheaper option wrong. It shows you what you're choosing.

> 💡 For each saving, ask what you give up with it and when that would hurt.
