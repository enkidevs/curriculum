# Stress-Testing The Recommendation

---
## Content

Before finalizing the printer purchase, ask AI to challenge the case without inventing new facts.

```plain-text
Using only this case, find weak assumptions, contrary evidence, overlooked alternatives, and missing facts that could change the decision. Label unsupported points as questions.
```

Suppose it asks whether demand is concentrated in two months and whether subcontracting that peak would cost less.

Those are not findings yet. Check monthly job records and get a subcontracting quote.

Then answer the point in the case, add a condition to the recommendation, or change it if the evidence says so.

**The point is to test the recommendation, and change it if it doesn't hold up.**

> 💡 Treat each challenge AI raises as a question to check in your own records.
