# Structuring A Business Case

---
## Content

Say a print shop's notes mix jobs it turned away, overtime, a quote for a new printer, and a nearby subcontractor.

**A business case should let the reader judge four things: the problem, available options, the recommendation, and its risks.**

Start by organizing the notes without filling gaps:

```plain-text
Using only these notes, draft sections for Problem, Options, Recommendation, and Risks. Label each statement as a fact, assumption, or missing evidence. Do not fill gaps.
```

The notes support buying a printer and continuing with overtime. Subcontracting stays a possible option until its price and capacity are confirmed.

If one option is fully worked out and the others are vague, the case looks decided before anyone reads it.

> 💡 Keep every option in the case, and mark what each one is still missing.
