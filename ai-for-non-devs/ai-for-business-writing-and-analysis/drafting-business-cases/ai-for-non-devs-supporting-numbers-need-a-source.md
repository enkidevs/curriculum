# Supporting Numbers Need A Source

---
## Content

The printer costs $58,000. The draft says its payback period[1] is fourteen months, but no source or calculation supports that figure.

A quote and a job log aren't enough to work that out.

Keep a list of every number in the case. **For each one, note its source, calculation, assumptions, owner, and whether it's checked.**

A payback estimate may need:

- purchase, installation, staffing, and running costs
- expected demand over time
- the profit after running costs, not just extra sales
- assumptions about capacity and downtime

AI can organize the inputs and the formula, but it shouldn't fill in missing numbers.

Have whoever owns the numbers check the method and inputs before the estimate goes into the recommendation.

> ‼️ Calling an invented number an "estimate" doesn't make it evidence.
