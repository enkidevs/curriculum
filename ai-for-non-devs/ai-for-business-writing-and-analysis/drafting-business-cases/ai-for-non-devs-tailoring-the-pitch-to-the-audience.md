# Tailoring The Pitch To The Audience

---
## Content

The finance director and production manager need the same printer case through different routes.

| Finance leads with | Operations leads with |
|---|---|
| Upfront cost | Capacity problem |
| Assumptions | Staffing |
| Downside forecast | Training, maintenance |
| Cash timing | Rollout |

**The order and depth change. The evidence, forecast range, unknowns, and material risks do not.**

Both versions still mention a staffing problem or a weak demand forecast, since either could change the decision. One reader may just get less detail.

> 💡 Tell AI what each reader owns and which questions they will ask, then ask it to keep every fact that could change the decision.
