# Checking The Arithmetic

---
## Content

The draft says weekly orders rose 9%, using source values of 1,240 and 1,390.

First check that both inputs were copied from the right rows, periods, and stores.

Then recalculate the derived figure with a calculator or spreadsheet:

```plain-text
(1,390 - 1,240) / 1,240 × 100 = 12.1%
```

Check labels as carefully as arithmetic. A rise from 4% to 11% is seven percentage points, not seven percent.

**Asking AI to show its working helps you check it, but redo the calculation yourself.**

> ‼️ Check three things: the numbers copied in, the result, and what the result is called.
