# Plain English Explanations Of Data

---
## Content

A three-week pilot of a wider delivery radius across twelve stores is compared with the previous three weeks:

```plain-text
Average weekly orders: 1,240 before; 1,390 during pilot
Average delivery time: 24 minutes before; 30 during pilot
Late deliveries: 4% before; 11% during pilot
```

**Turning each line into a sentence is not yet an explanation.**

For a store manager, name the pattern:

```plain-text
Orders were higher during the pilot, but deliveries took longer and were more often late. Weekly orders rose about 12%, average delivery time increased by six minutes, and late deliveries rose from 4% to 11%.
```

This says what changed without claiming that the wider radius caused every change.

> ‼️ A common mistake is saying the pilot caused the changes. The numbers only show they happened together.

---
## Practice

A clinic trial shows bookings up 18%, waits up from 9 to 15 minutes, and missed appointments up from 3% to 8%. Sum up the pattern in one sentence.

???

- Bookings rose, but patients waited longer and missed more appointments.
