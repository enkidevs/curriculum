# Flagging Risks Honestly

---
## Content

The source note says racking is three weeks late and go-live may slip.

The draft says:

```plain-text
Racking is progressing against a revised timeline.
```

That wording hides the delay and its consequence.

A useful risk entry has four parts:

- current fact or uncertainty
- possible impact and trigger
- owner and mitigation
- next checkpoint or decision date

Here's the racking risk written that way:

```plain-text
Racking is three weeks late. If installation is not complete by April 18, the May 3 go-live is at risk. The supplier's recovery plan is due April 12.
```

**After a rewrite, check the risk still says how serious it is and what it affects.**

> ‼️ A common mistake is leaving out the next checkpoint. Readers need to know when they'll hear more.
