# Keeping Updates Consistent Over Time

---
## Content

Four polished monthly updates can still make progress hard to compare.

Headings move. Milestones get renamed. One month uses dates and another uses weeks. Risks vanish without anyone saying they're resolved.

Give AI last month's approved update along with this month's notes, and ask it to keep the same:

- headings
- milestone names
- order
- units
- meaning for each status

For every changed date, show the previous and current date.

If a milestone is renamed, show the old name next to the new one so its history stays easy to follow.

**A stable template makes changes, omissions, and recurring problems easier to see and check.**

> 💡 Carry unresolved risks and decisions forward until someone records an outcome.

---
## Practice

What should stay the same across monthly status updates so progress is easy to compare?

???

- The headings, milestone names, units, and what each status means.

---
## Revision

A milestone is genuinely renamed this month. How do you keep its history readable?

???

- Show a mapping from the old name to the new one.
