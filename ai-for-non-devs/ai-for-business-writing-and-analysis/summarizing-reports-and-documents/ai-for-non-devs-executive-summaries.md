# Executive Summaries

---
## Content

Say executives must decide whether to renew a provider, based on a 55-page review.

**An executive summary leads with what the evidence means for that decision, not the report's section order.** Here's a request built around the decision:

```plain-text
Write a one-page summary for executives deciding whether to renew this provider. Open with what the evidence implies, including uncertainty. Then give the three findings behind it, the options, major costs and risks, and the next action. Add a source location for every figure.
```

If the evidence does not support one clear answer, the summary should say so.

Shortening every section evenly just gives you a smaller report.

> 💡 Tell AI who reads the summary and what they have to decide.
