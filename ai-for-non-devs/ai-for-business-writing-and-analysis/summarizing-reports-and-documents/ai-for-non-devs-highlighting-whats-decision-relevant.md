# Highlighting What Affects The Decision

---
## Content

**The most surprising finding in a report isn't always the one the decision needs first.**

Say you're deciding whether to renew a training provider. The things you've agreed to judge it on might be learner outcomes, total cost, delivery against commitments, and transition risk.

Then ask AI to sort the report's findings into four groups:

1. supports renewal
2. argues against renewal
3. adds uncertainty or needs follow-up
4. is background only

Ask it to give the page for each finding, then check where it put each one yourself.

AI can help sort the evidence. It shouldn't pick what you judge on, drop findings that disagree, or make the decision.

> 💡 Start with the decision and its agreed criteria, then sort the findings by what they mean for it.
