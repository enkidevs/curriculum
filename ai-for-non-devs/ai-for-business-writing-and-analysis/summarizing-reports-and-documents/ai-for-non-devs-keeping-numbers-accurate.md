# Keeping Numbers Accurate

---
## Content

The summary says the program cost $304,000. The report says $340,000.

Both look believable, so the mistake can get past a careful reread.

**Every number in the summary needs checking against its source**, and more than the digits:

- value and unit or currency
- time period
- who or what it counts
- what was measured or compared

Asking AI to add a page or table for each figure speeds up the search, but the page can be wrong too. Open the source and confirm it yourself.

If you can't match a figure, mark it unchecked, then fix it or remove it.

> ‼️ A common mistake is checking only the digits. $340,000 for one year and $340,000 for three years are different numbers.
