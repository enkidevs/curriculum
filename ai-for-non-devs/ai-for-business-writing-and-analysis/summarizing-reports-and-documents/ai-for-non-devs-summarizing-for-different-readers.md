# Summarizing For Different Readers

---
## Content

The executive team and training manager need different summaries of the same review.

| Executives need | Training manager needs |
|---|---|
| Outcomes | Teaching |
| Cost | Assessment |
| Major risks | Scheduling |
| Meaning for renewal | Mentor time |

The manager gets more detail because those are the areas they can change.

The facts stay the same. What's included, the order, and the depth change.

Keep any caveat that changes what a finding means. **Tailoring a summary does not mean hiding evidence one reader may dislike.**

> 💡 Write separate requests that name each reader, what they're responsible for, and what they can do about it.

---
## Practice

Executives decide whether to renew a training program; its manager runs scheduling and mentoring. Whose summary goes deeper on scheduling?

???

- The training manager's, because they can change those areas.
