# How The Same Reader Uses Each Channel

---
## Content

One customer may meet the same launch several times.

They scan a social post, judge a subject line in a crowded inbox, then open the email when they want detail.

The person is the same. What they're doing, and how much attention they have, is different. You can tell AI how that person uses each channel:

```plain-text
The social post is scanned mid-scroll, so lead with one relevant benefit. The email is opened for details, so explain the setup and next step there.
```

**That changes what goes first and how much detail each version has. The voice and the customer stay the same.**

> 💡 Put the full details where the reader stops to read.
