# Ignoring Channel Constraints

---
## Content

Say AI drafts twelve product cards before anyone opens the catalog template.

The cards use long paragraphs. The template has a short title, a forty-word body, and no formatting.

Now every card needs the same rework.

**Testing one item first catches this before it spreads**:

1. Get the real limits from the destination or its owner.
2. Draft one representative item.
3. Place it in the actual template and check the fit.
4. Fix the prompt, then produce the batch.

A general request like "keep it concise" can't catch a forty-word limit.

> 💡 Before drafting a batch, put one finished item into the real template and check that it fits.
