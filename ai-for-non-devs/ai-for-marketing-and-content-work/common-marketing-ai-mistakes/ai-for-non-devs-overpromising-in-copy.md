# Overpromising In Copy

---
## Content

The brief says a bike lock has a hardened-steel body and weighs three pounds.

The draft says:

```plain-text
Unbreakable security wherever you park.
```

"Unbreakable" sounds like enthusiasm, but it's a promise about performance the brief doesn't support.

**Every product claim needs an approved fact behind it.** Hardened steel supports a claim about what the lock is made of. It doesn't prove nobody can break it.

You can set that limit before drafting:

```plain-text
Use only documented capabilities. Do not turn materials into performance guarantees.
```

The strongest line you can back up is simpler:

```plain-text
Hardened-steel body in a three-pound lock.
```

> ‼️ A common mistake is turning a material into a guarantee, like steel into "unbreakable" or water-resistant into "waterproof".
