# Publishing Unverified Stats

---
## Content

A social post claims that sixty-eight percent of small firms lose a day each week to admin.

The only place the number appears is the AI draft.

A precise-looking number is easy to repeat, screenshot, and quote. If it's wrong, fixing the post won't fix the screenshots people already shared.

**Before publication, a statistic needs a credible source that supports its exact percentage, audience, measure, timeframe, and scope.**

If that source does not exist, find verified evidence and rewrite the claim, or remove the number.

Calling it "about sixty-eight percent" or adding "according to research" does not create evidence.

> ‼️ If the number still has no source when the post is due, hold the post or cut the number.
