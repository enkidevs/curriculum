# Treating A First Draft As Final

---
## Content

A polished first draft can still be wrong, incomplete, off-brief, or unsuitable for its channel.

Smooth prose makes those problems easier to skim past.

**Before it's final, the job changes from polishing the wording to checking it**:

- check it against the brief and source material
- mark every fact and promise
- test the offer, dates, names, and links
- confirm the brand and channel requirements
- complete the normal approval process

AI can help list what to check. Its confident wording isn't evidence, and the decision to publish stays with you and your approvers.

> 💡 Even when every check passes, the draft still goes through normal approval before it goes out.
