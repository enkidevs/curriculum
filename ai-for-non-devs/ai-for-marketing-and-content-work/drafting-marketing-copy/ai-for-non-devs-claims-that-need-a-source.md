# Mark Claims Before They Hide

---
## Content

Polished copy can hide claims that never appeared in the brief. You can ask AI to mark every statistic, guarantee, comparison, or superlative your material doesn't support:

```plain-text
Cuts 40% faster than the leading model. [SOURCE NEEDED: comparison]
Built to last a lifetime. [SOURCE NEEDED: guarantee]
Trusted by over 50,000 gardeners. [SOURCE NEEDED: customer count]
```

**A marker doesn't make a claim true. It stops the claim from hiding in a draft that sounds finished.**

Find a real source for each marked line. If there isn't one, cut it or swap in a fact from the brief.

> ‼️ A common mistake is trusting a claim because it's precise, like "40% faster". If the draft is its only source, it's invented.
