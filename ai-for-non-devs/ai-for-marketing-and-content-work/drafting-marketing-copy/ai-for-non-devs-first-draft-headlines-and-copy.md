# Start From The Brief

---
## Content

**A useful first draft starts with the product brief, not an empty request for something catchy.**

The brief gives AI the facts the copy can use: audience, features, offer, proof, and format limits. 

Here's a prompt that uses one:

```plain-text
Using only facts in this brief, write six product-page headlines under ten words.

BRIEF: Cordless hedge trimmer. Five pounds. Forty-five-minute runtime. $129 with battery. For people with one hedge and limited storage.
```

It might come back with:

```plain-text
Five pounds. Forty-five cordless minutes.
Cordless trimming, battery included.
```

These are still drafts, but every useful part comes from the brief instead of stock marketing phrases.

> 💡 When the copy comes back generic, add the brief.
