# Matching Brand Voice

---
## Content

A voice label like this leaves AI a lot of room to guess:

```plain-text
VOICE: Warm but understated.
```

It can still give you "Say hello to dinner, reinvented!"

**Two or three approved lines from the brand show AI what the label means.** Here's the label replaced with examples:

```plain-text
Match the examples' short sentences, plain vocabulary, and restrained energy. Do not copy product details or claims.

EXAMPLES:
Dinner, sorted. Six ingredients and one pan.
Good food. Fewer decisions.
```

The examples show what "understated" means here, and the first line says what to copy and what to leave out.

> 💡 Pick examples in the same format as the copy you need.
