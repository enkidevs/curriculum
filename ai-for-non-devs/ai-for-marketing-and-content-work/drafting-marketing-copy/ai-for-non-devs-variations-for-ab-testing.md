# Variations Worth Comparing

---
## Content

More versions help only when they differ in a way worth comparing. Six subject lines that just swap synonyms or punctuation are really one idea six times.

**Useful variations change one thing on purpose and keep the audience, offer, channel, and limits the same.** Here's a prompt that does that:

```plain-text
Write four subject lines for the same January gym-membership email. Keep each under 45 characters. Use one angle per line: time saved, price, flexibility, or a fresh start.
```

Each line now tests a different angle, so you can compare them.

> ‼️ A common mistake is changing the angle and the offer in one version. Then you can't tell which one worked.
