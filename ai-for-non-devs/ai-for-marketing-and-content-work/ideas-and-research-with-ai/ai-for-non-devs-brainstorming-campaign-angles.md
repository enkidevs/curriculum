# Brainstorming Campaign Angles

---
## Content

Ask AI for twelve campaign ideas with no direction, and many will be the same idea reworded. **More ideas don't give you more variety.**

Naming the directions does:

```plain-text
Using only the trail-shoe brief, generate twelve one-line campaign angles, three around each of these:
- a customer problem
- a use moment
- an objection
- a category assumption to challenge
Label each group. Do not write final copy.
```

These are ideas to judge, not finished campaigns or proof of what customers want. Check them against the brief, your research, the proof you have, and the brand.

> 💡 Name the directions the ideas should explore, then judge them against the brief.
