# Verify Competitor And Market Claims

---
## Content

AI can help list research questions or structure a comparison. Its answer isn't proof of a current price, feature, market size, or growth rate.

An answer that used web search may list sources. Those sources can still be out of date or misread.

For a competitor's price or published feature, check their official page and its date.

For market figures, use research that names its publisher, date, method, region, and what exactly it counted.

A link alone is not enough. **Confirm that the page supports the exact number and scope being used.**

> ‼️ "According to industry reports" is not a source. Which report, measuring what, and when?
