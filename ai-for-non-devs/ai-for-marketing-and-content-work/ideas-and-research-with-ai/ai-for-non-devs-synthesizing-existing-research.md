# Synthesizing Existing Research

---
## Content

Research rarely arrives as one clean document.

You may have a workshop transcript, survey results, sales notes, and an old positioning document.

**AI can pull them into one brief, as long as every point still shows where it came from**:

```plain-text
Create a one-page brief with Evidence, Interpretation, and Open Questions. Label every point with its source. Show disagreements instead of resolving them.
```

Labels let you check any line that surprises you. Keeping evidence apart from interpretation shows where the brief goes beyond what people actually said.

> ‼️ A common mistake is letting AI merge two sources that disagree into one confident line. Ask it to show both views and flag them as open.
