# Checking Facts And Claims

---
## Content

A customer story draft says a design studio won thirty percent more bids after changing its quoting process.

**That's a claim, and it needs checking however believable it sounds.**

AI can help pull checkable claims into a list. Then:

1. Next to each one, note its source and whether it's been checked.
2. Open the source.
3. Confirm that it supports the exact number, subject, time period, and comparison in the draft.

If the studio's records show thirty percent more inquiries, they do not support thirty percent more won bids.

> 💡 When the source measures something close but different, rewrite the claim to match the source.

---
## Practice

An AI customer story says a studio "won 30% more bids." What must the studio's records show?

???

- 30% more won bids, over the same period, not some other measure.

## Revision

A studio's records show 30% more inquiries, but the AI draft says "30% more won bids." Rewrite the claim.
