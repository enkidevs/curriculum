# Getting Feedback Before You Publish

---
## Content

**A fast draft still follows the same approval process as any other draft.**

Your own reread can catch problems. It does not replace a required editor, subject expert, manager, or compliance reviewer.

A useful handoff note gives the reviewer the brief or source, what you checked, what's still open, and what you need them to decide. For example:

```plain-text
Checked pricing and dates against the product sheet. The customer result in paragraph three is still unverified. Please review the claim and the final call to action.
```

> 💡 Name the unchecked claim in your note, so the reviewer knows exactly where to look.
