# Losing Brand Consistency

---
## Content

**Each newsletter can look acceptable on its own while the brand voice slowly drifts.**

Across several months, sentences may grow longer, familiar humor may disappear, or every call to action may start sounding the same.

Two habits help:

- **Fixed voice guide:** start every draft from the same guide, built from approved examples.
- **Batch review:** every few months, read several published pieces side by side.

If each draft starts from last month's AI-written issue, small changes pile up. In the batch, compare openings, vocabulary, sentence length, formality, humor, and calls to action.

When something drifts, fix the guide using approved examples.

> 💡 To spot drift, compare issues from months apart, not just this month's draft against last month's.
