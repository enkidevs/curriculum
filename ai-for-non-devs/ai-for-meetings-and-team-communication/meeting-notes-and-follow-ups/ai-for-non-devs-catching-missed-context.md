# Catching What The Notes Missed

---
## Content

The notes say the group agreed on June.

They omit what everyone heard next: June works only if the Q3 budget is approved, and nobody has requested approval.

A draft built from those notes will call June settled. **AI cannot recover a condition it never received.**

Check the follow-up against what you remember and, if you're allowed to use it, the recording or transcript.

Look for conditions someone said out loud, exceptions, disagreements nobody settled, and missing owners. Add what matters by hand, and mark anything you can't confirm.

> ‼️ Asking AI "what did the notes miss?" won't help here. It can only see what's in the notes.

---
## Practice

Notes say the June date was agreed; in the room it depended on Q3 budget approval. The AI follow-up calls June settled. What do you fix?

???

- Show June as depending on budget approval, not settled.

## Revision
