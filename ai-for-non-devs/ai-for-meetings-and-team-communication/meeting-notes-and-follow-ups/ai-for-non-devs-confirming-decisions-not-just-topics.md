# Decisions, Not Just Topics

---
## Content

"We discussed the returns period" records a topic, not an outcome.

**A useful decision record says what changes, when, under which condition, and what happens next**:

```plain-text
Conditional decision: Returns move from 14 to 30 days on September 1 if the carrier confirms support. Priya checks by Friday. If support is unavailable, the period remains 14 days.

Open question: Whether refunds return to the original payment method.
```

A meeting can decide, decide with a condition, put off a decision, or leave a question open. Say which one happened.

Have the meeting owner or attendees check the decision list. Ask for corrections, but no reply doesn't mean everyone agreed.

> ‼️ A common mistake is writing "agreed" for something the group only discussed. Record it as an open question with a next step.
