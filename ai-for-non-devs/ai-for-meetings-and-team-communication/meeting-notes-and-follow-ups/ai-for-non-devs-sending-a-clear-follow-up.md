# Sending A Clear Follow-Up

---
## Content

Six people receiving the returns follow-up were not on the call.

**They need enough context to understand the result, not a replay of the discussion.**

Give AI the audience and a fixed structure:

```plain-text
Draft for attendees and absent recipients. Start with two lines on why the returns period was reviewed. Then list confirmed or conditional decisions, actions with owners and dates, and open questions. Mark unassigned or undated actions. Use only these checked notes.
```

Put links or source documents beside the item they support.

Before sending, verify every name, date, decision, and condition. Ask for corrections, but don't take silence as agreement.

> 💡 Open with a line or two on why the topic came up, so people who missed the meeting understand the change.
