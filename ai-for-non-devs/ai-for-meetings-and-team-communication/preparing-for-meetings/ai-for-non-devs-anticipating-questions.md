# Anticipating The Questions

---
## Content

Knowing what each person is responsible for helps you guess their questions. It doesn't tell you what they think.

For Saturday opening, finance may need cost and demand evidence. The head vet may need rota and on-call coverage. Clinic managers may need staffing and demand.

**AI can list the questions people may ask, but it can't know who will object**:

```plain-text
Using only these responsibilities and notes, list questions each role may need answered. Explain why each is relevant. Mark assumptions. Beside each question, show the evidence we have, what is missing, and who could answer it.
```

An unanswered question, like who covers Saturday on-call, is work to do before the meeting.

Keep "may ask" from turning into "will oppose". That gives someone a view they never stated.

> 💡 For each likely question, find the evidence or who can answer it.
