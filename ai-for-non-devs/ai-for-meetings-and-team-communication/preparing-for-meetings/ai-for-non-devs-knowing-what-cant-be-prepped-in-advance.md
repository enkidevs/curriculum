# What You Can't Prep For

---
## Content

Ten minutes into the meeting, the head vet says they are leaving in March.

That new fact may change the staffing assumptions behind Saturday opening.

Pause and say what's changed:

1. Confirm the new information and timing.
2. Name which assumptions or options it affects.
3. Decide whether to keep going with a narrower question, change what the meeting covers, or put off the decision.
4. Give someone the job of getting any missing evidence.

The meeting may still reach an outcome, though not the original one.

**Good prep leaves room to notice a shift like this, instead of pushing everyone back to the agenda.**

> ‼️ A common mistake is finishing the agenda after a key assumption has changed. Stop and decide what the meeting can still settle.
