# Talking Points, Not A Script

---
## Content

A live discussion rarely follows the order of a prepared speech.

**Separate talking points let you follow the discussion wherever it goes.** Give each one:

- one claim or position
- the evidence and source behind it
- what you don't know yet
- a question for the group
- its link to the meeting decision

Here's the request:

```plain-text
Create five independent talking points for Saturday opening. No paragraphs or transitions. Include the fields above.
```

If staffing comes up before demand, you go straight to that point.

> 💡 Give every point a line for what you don't know yet, like the cost of extra staffing.
