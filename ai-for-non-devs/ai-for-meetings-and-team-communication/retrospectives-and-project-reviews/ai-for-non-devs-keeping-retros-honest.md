# Keeping Retros Honest

---
## Content

Nine notes about late client paperwork become one theme:

```plain-text
Client onboarding could be handled more consistently.
```

The theme is so broad that nobody can check or challenge it.

**Each theme needs its evidence underneath it**:

- how many source notes support it
- the dates or period covered
- one concrete example
- any exception or disagreement
- links back to the source notes

Here's the theme rewritten with its evidence:

```plain-text
Theme: Late paperwork kept intake open past the planned cutoff.
Evidence: Nine notes from January 8–20. One office said its reminders worked.
Example: Intake stayed open until January 20.
```

> 💡 A count shows how often something came up, not how serious it is.
