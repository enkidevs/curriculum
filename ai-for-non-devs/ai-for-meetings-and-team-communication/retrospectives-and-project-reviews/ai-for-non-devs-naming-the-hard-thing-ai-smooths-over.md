# Naming The Thing The Draft Smoothed Over

---
## Content

The retro says "resourcing was tight in the final two weeks."

The notes say two people each worked three weekends and no redistribution decision followed the warning.

Replace the soft wording with what actually happened:

```plain-text
Two people each worked three weekends in the final two weeks. No redistribution decision was recorded after the issue was raised.
```

One of them later resigned. Nothing links that to the workload, so report it as a separate fact.

Leave out names the team doesn't need to fix the problem.

Being direct doesn't mean blaming anyone. **Say what happened, what it caused, and what was done about it, without guessing at motives.**

> ‼️ Don't soften what happened, and don't claim more than the notes show.
