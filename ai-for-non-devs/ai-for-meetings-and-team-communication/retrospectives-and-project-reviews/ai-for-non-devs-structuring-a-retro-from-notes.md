# Structuring A Retro From Notes

---
## Content

Tax-season debrief notes mix observations, disagreements, and a few proposed actions.

**AI can sort them without filling in the rest of the story**:

```plain-text
Sort into Went Well, Could Improve, and Proposed Next Steps. Add source IDs. Merge only clear duplicates. Preserve disagreements. Do not invent actions. List uncertain items separately.
```

AI may reword and group related points. The source IDs let you check each one against what was written.

"Nobody wanted to turn a late client away" is an observation. It does not become "Introduce a cutoff date" unless someone proposed that action.

Review the grouping with the team before treating it as the shared retro.

> ‼️ A common mistake is keeping an action AI made up from an observation. Leave it as a problem until someone proposes a fix.
