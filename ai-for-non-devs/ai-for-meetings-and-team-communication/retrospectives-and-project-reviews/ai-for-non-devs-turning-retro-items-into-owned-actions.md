# Turning Retro Items Into Owned Actions

---
## Content

"Manage capacity better" repeats the problem without creating work anyone can track.

A real action names:

- the task or decision
- what completion looks like
- an owner who accepted it
- a due date
- where progress will be reviewed

```plain-text
Review last season's weekly workload and propose a capacity threshold. Owner: unassigned. Due: March 15. Review: March partners' meeting.
```

**AI can draft that shape, but the team must confirm its owner.**

Some retro items need a group decision rather than an individual task. A rule for turning late clients away belongs with the partners, since they're the ones who can set it.

> 💡 Leave the owner as "unassigned" until someone actually accepts the action.
