# Agreeing What Stays Human

---
## Content

**Agree which messages stay human-written before a hard one comes up.**

Examples include:

- performance or disciplinary feedback
- redundancy or role-change news
- a serious apology that accepts responsibility
- condolences
- consequential bad news or conflict

These messages do more than pass on information. The person sending them is making a judgment, taking responsibility for the impact, or looking after a relationship.

Under a human-written rule, that person starts from a blank page and chooses every word. They still follow any required HR, legal, or leadership review.

Don't paste sensitive personal details into AI to get around it.

> 💡 Write the list of human-only messages, with examples and who to ask, into the team policy.
