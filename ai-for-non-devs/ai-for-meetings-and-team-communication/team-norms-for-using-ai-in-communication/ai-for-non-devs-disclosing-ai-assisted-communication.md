# Saying When A Message Was AI-Drafted

---
## Content

A useful team norm is more specific than "always disclose" or "never disclose."

First, follow any rule from your organization, a client contract, or the law.

**Then ask whether knowing AI helped would change how the reader takes the message.** Disclosure may matter when:

- personal effort is the point, like a thank-you note
- AI did real analysis or made a recommendation
- the reader asked for work written by a person

Agree whether help with spelling or formatting counts.

When you do disclose it, be specific:

```plain-text
AI helped organize the first draft. I checked the source notes and approved the final message.
```

> ‼️ Saying AI helped doesn't replace checking the message.
