# Reviewing What Goes Out Under Your Name

---
## Content

A draft that already sounds like you is easy to skim.

**Before sending, check what the message actually says, however good it sounds.** Check:

- who gets it and who else can see it
- facts, sources, dates, and numbers
- promises, approvals, and requested actions
- confidential or personal information
- tone and meaning for this reader
- policy and disclosure requirements

Open any linked source. Confirm that every commitment is one you can make and every action has the right owner.

If you cannot explain or defend a sentence, change or remove it.

AI can help write the checklist, but you do the final review, even if you say AI helped.

> ‼️ A common mistake is sending a promise nobody approved, like a delivery date.
