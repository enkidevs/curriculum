# What Agents Are Useful For Now

---
## Content

**Good first jobs for an agent have a clear end, several steps, and results you can check**:

- **Gathering:** collect the same three facts from twelve supplier pages, with a source beside every row.
- **Processing:** sort a batch of receipts by supplier and printed date while keeping the originals.
- **Cross-app chores:** add approved trade-show leads to a spreadsheet and flag incomplete records.

These jobs have a clear finish. You can check the sources, look at a sample of results, and fix a mistake.

Leave big decisions and anything hard to undo, like emailing clients, until the agent has worked well on smaller jobs.

> 💡 Pick a first job where you can check every result against a source.
