# What Makes Something An Agent

---
## Content

An assistant can answer a question or write a plan. **An agent works through the steps needed to reach a goal.**

It chooses the next step, uses the tools it has, checks what happened, and adjusts. It keeps going until the task is done, it gets stuck, or it needs you.

Ask it to schedule a supplier visit and it might check calendars, find an allowed time, draft the invitation, then pause before sending it.

Pausing doesn't make it less of an agent. It still worked out the steps itself, and you kept the final say on sending.

> 💡 To tell if a tool is an agent, ask whether it picks its own next step.
