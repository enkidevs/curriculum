# Models And Products

---
## Content

The **model** is the AI that reads your prompt and writes the reply.

The **product** is the app or service around it: the chat screen, saved conversations, file uploads, search, memory, and usage limits.

Think of the model as an engine and the product as the vehicle built around it. **One model can power several products, and one product can offer several models.**

ChatGPT[1], Claude.ai[2], and the Gemini app[3] are products. Claude and Gemini are also the names of model families, which is why the names get mixed up.

> 💡 When an assistant starts behaving differently, check whether the model changed or the app around it did.
