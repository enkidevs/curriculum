# When Search Is Still Better

---
## Content

Start with search or the official site when you need:

- **Current facts:** a new fee, a recent rule, or today's opening hours
- **A source you can cite:** something another person may ask you to prove
- **One official answer:** a regulator's rule, a government form, or a supplier's terms

An assistant with web access can help find these pages. **Treat its links as leads: open the original page and check the claim there.**

Once you have the official text, an assistant can work with it, like turning guidance into a staff checklist.

> 💡 If someone may ask where a fact came from, get it from the official page and keep the link.
