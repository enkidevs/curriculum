# When ChatGPT Fits

---
## Content

Three kinds of work suit ChatGPT's range:

- **Mixed media:** turn a photographed whiteboard into organized notes, then draft the follow-up.
- **File and data work:** read a spreadsheet, calculate grouped totals, and create a chart.[1]
- **One-off tasks:** compare several sources, draft a response, or reshape material you won't need again.

When a suitable connected app is available, ChatGPT can also bring information from another service into the conversation. Availability depends on the account and workspace.[2]

**Pick it when your account has the feature the task needs.**

> 💡 ChatGPT fits best when a task moves between formats, like a photo that becomes notes and then an email.
