# What ChatGPT's Range Buys You

---
## Content

ChatGPT's[1] main strength is range: one chat can handle many kinds of work.

One conversation can move from an uploaded spreadsheet to analysis and a chart, then to a written explanation and an image. Voice, web search, and connected apps may also be available.[2]

**That helps when a task keeps changing format.** You stay in one place instead of picking a different tool for each step.

> 💡 Check each result for what its format needs, like the numbers behind a chart or the facts in an email.
