# Projects And Artifacts Do Different Jobs

---
## Content

A **Project** groups related chats, instructions, and knowledge files. Conversations inside it can use that project context without you uploading the same brief each time.[1]

An **Artifact** gives a larger piece of work its own space beside the chat. You can keep improving a document, diagram, tool, or other standalone result without burying every version in the thread.[2]

**They cover the two ends of the work:**

- **Project:** the material and rules you begin with
- **Artifact:** the thing you are building from them

Features and sharing options vary by account, plan, app, and settings.

> 💡 If you upload the same files and instructions every week, put them in a Project.
