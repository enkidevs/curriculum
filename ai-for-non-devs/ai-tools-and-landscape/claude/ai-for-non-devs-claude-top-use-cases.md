# Start With The Source Pack

---
## Content

Claude is a good choice when the result has to stay tied to your documents the whole way through:

- **Compare:** read three annual reports and show what changed, with a source beside each finding.
- **Extract:** turn a long consultation transcript into decisions, owners, and unresolved questions.
- **Rewrite:** use a policy and several approved writing samples to produce a new version without changing key terms.

Claude supports document uploads[1], and Projects keep your instructions next to your documents.

**What these share is that every part of the result has to trace back to long documents, while following the same writing instructions.**

> 💡 Pick Claude when you'll revise several times and every version has to stay true to the same documents.
