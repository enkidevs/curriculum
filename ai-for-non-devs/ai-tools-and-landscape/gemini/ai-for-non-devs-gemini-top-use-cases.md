# Where Gemini Earns Its Place

---
## Content

**Gemini fits best when the material and the finished work both live in Google Workspace.**

Good fits include:

- summarizing the document already open
- finding decisions across named emails or files
- updating a draft from specified Workspace sources
- exploring or organizing data in a Sheet[1]

For repeated work, use it for first drafts you can check quickly, like sorting routine emails, drafting variations, or pulling the same fields from similar files.

**The quick check matters, because at high volume one unchecked mistake can repeat hundreds of times.**

> 💡 Before handing Gemini a big batch, make sure each result takes seconds to check.
