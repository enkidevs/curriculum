# When the Work Is Already in Google

---
## Content

Gemini's biggest advantage is where it works.

In supported Google Workspace[1] apps, Gemini[2] can work beside the document, email, or spreadsheet already open.[3]

It can also use files, emails, and other Workspace sources you point it to. That saves downloading, uploading, and copying them into another tool.

**The benefit is practical: less setup before summarizing a document, refining a draft, or pulling actions from a thread.**

It doesn't read everything on its own. You still say what to do and, when needed, which file or email to use.

> 💡 Pick Gemini when the files and emails a task needs are already in Google Workspace.
