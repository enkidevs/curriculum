# Connecting It To Your Tools

---
## Content

Without a connection, an assistant can use only what its product provides or what you add.

Connected apps can let it search a drive, read a calendar, or use another service. Some can also take actions you've allowed, like creating a calendar event.

The labels vary: apps, connectors, integrations, or plugins.

You may also see **MCP**, short for Model Context Protocol[1]. It is an open standard for connecting AI products to outside data and tools. Not every connection uses it.

If you upload the same files again and again, a connection can remove that step. **It does not remove the need to check the answer.**

> 💡 If you upload the same files every week, look for a connection to where they're stored.
