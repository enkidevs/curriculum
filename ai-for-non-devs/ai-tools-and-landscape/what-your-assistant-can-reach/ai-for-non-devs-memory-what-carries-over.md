# What Can Carry Between Chats

---
## Content

Inside one chat, earlier messages are part of its current context.

Between chats, the product may add saved memories, past chats, custom instructions, or project context.

**Memory is a product feature, not the model recalling every conversation.** What's available depends on the product, account, and settings.

That saves you repeating yourself, but old details can mislead. An audience preference from an old project may shape an unrelated draft months later.

If the same wrong assumption shows up in new chats, check the memory settings. Correct it, delete it, or use a chat mode that doesn't use memory.

> ‼️ A new chat clears the thread, but it may not clear what the product has saved about you.
