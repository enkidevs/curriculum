# Matching The Tool To The Task

---
## Content

No assistant wins every version of a task. **Start with the one that removes the hardest part of the job.**

- If the sources already live in Google Workspace, Gemini saves gathering and copying them.
- If many long documents have to feed one piece of writing, Claude may fit.
- If the task moves among images, spreadsheets, charts, voice, and writing, ChatGPT saves switching tools.

These are starting points. All three overlap, and what you can use depends on your account and setup.

> 💡 Pick the tool that removes today's hardest step, then check what it actually produces.
