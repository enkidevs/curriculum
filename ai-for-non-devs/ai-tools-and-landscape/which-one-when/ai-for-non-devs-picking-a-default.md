# Picking A Default

---
## Content

**A default is a starting point, not a winner.**

Pick the assistant that fits the work you do most, where your files live, what your account includes, and your organization's rules.

| Kind of work | Likely default |
|---|---|
| Work inside Google Workspace | Gemini |
| Source-heavy drafting | Claude |
| Changing formats | ChatGPT[1] |

These are examples to adapt.

Write down when you'd switch: "When the task needs X, use Y." That way the default doesn't stop you from using a better tool.

Revisit the choice when your work, tools, or access changes.

> 💡 Note a switch rule for any task your default handles badly.
