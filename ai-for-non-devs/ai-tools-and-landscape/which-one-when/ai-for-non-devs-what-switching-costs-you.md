# What Switching Costs You

---
## Content

**Switching moves the task, not its setup.**

The new tool doesn't get your sources, uploaded files, earlier corrections, format choices, saved preferences, or connections.

You can carry some of that across by hand. Doing so takes time and creates a new risk: leaving out the instruction that made the result work.

Compare two things before moving:

1. what the new tool would let you do
2. the time and risk of setting everything up again

Switch when the first clearly outweighs the second. Otherwise, finish the task and use what you learned when choosing next time.

> 💡 Before switching mid-task, list what you'd have to set up again. If the list is long, finish where you are.
