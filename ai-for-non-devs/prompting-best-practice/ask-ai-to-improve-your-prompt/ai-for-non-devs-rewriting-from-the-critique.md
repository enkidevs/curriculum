# Acting On The Critique

---
## Content

**A critique changes nothing until you make the missing decisions and rewrite the prompt.**

Here's the interview example again, with your decisions added:

```plain-text
Rewrite the prompt using these decisions: eight questions, an operations coordinator with about two years' experience, a panel interview, and a focus on competing deadlines. Make two questions ask for a specific past example.
```

AI can now produce:

```plain-text
Write eight panel interview questions for an operations coordinator with about two years' experience. Focus on handling competing deadlines. Make two questions ask for a specific past example.
```

> 💡 Review the rewrite once. Keep only the decisions you actually made.
