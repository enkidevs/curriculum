# Summaries Instead Of Full Documents

---
## Content

**If a task depends on a document's structure rather than its exact wording, describe the structure instead of pasting the document.**

```plain-text
I have a six-page consultancy proposal with three pricing tiers, a 90-day timeline, and a separate statement of work for any scope change. Give me eight questions to ask before signing, focused on what happens if the timeline slips.
```

This shares less while keeping the pricing, timeline, and scope details needed for useful questions.

When exact wording is the problem, use the smallest relevant extract, and only if your workplace allows it in that tool.

> 💡 Before you paste a long document, try describing it in two or three sentences.
