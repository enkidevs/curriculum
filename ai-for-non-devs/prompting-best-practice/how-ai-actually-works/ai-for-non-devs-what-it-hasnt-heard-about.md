# Knowledge Cutoffs

---
## Content

AI models are trained on information collected up to a certain point. That boundary is called a knowledge cutoff.

The world keeps changing after training stops. Ask about a shipping rule that changed six weeks ago and you may get the previous rule described in the present tense.

The answer will not announce that its knowledge may be old. **If the detail could have changed, check the current source before using it.**

> ‼️ Specific dates and confident wording do not make an unsourced answer current.

---
## Practice

A shipping rule changed six weeks ago. AI describes it in detail with no source. Where do you check it?

???

- Against the current rule on the regulator's own site.

---
## Revision

An answer quotes this year's rates but gives no links. What do you need before relying on it?

???

- The current figure from the organization that publishes it.
