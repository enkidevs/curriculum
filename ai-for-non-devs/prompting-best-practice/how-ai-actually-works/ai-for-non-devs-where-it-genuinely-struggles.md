# Calculations And Logic

---
## Content

AI can draft a clear expense summary and still get the total wrong.

Give it 34 claims to add up. It may work through them correctly, but an arithmetic slip can survive into an answer that reads just as confidently as a right one.

**For exact arithmetic, use a calculator or spreadsheet.** AI can still help explain the checked result.

Use the same caution for counting or for rules that must all fit at once.

> 💡 Looking right is not the same as being right when the result has to be exact.
