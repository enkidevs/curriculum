# Checking What Is Missing

---
## Content

**A useful follow-up is to ask AI to challenge the answer it just gave you.**

For a first-draft checklist about moving 40 people to a new floor, ask:

```plain-text
What is missing from this checklist that would matter in the final week before the move? Name three gaps and explain why each matters.
```

This checks the answer you already have. A prompt critique happens earlier, before there's any answer.

> ‼️ This is a cheap second check, not verification. Important plans still need review by someone who understands the work.
