# Controlled Versions

---
## Content

One draft makes it hard to see the effect of a single choice. **Two controlled versions make that difference visible**:

```plain-text
Write two openings for the all-hands announcement. Lead the first with the decision and the second with the reason. Keep the length, tone, and facts the same.
```

Now you can judge which opening better fits your audience. You can also combine the stronger opening with a line from the other version.

> 💡 Change one thing at a time. If tone and length change too, you will not know what caused the difference.
