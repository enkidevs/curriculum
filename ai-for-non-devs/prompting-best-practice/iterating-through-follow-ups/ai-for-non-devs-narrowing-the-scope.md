# Narrowing The Scope

---
## Content

**When an answer sprawls, choose one useful part and ask for a more specific result.**

Suppose AI gives you 15 ideas for reducing missed appointments. Pick one:

```plain-text
Take idea four, a reminder call two days ahead. Give me the steps for a one-month trial, the owner of each step, and one measure of success.
```

> 💡 Giving up breadth gets you something you can test: steps, owners, and a measure.
