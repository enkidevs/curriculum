# Human, AI, Human

---
## Content

For sensitive work, use **human, AI, human**, if the tool is approved for the material.

- **Human:** write the facts, judgment, and required outcome.
- **AI:** make it clearer or change the tone without changing what you said.
- **Human:** verify every fact and approve the final wording.

For difficult performance feedback, you decide what happened, what must change, and by when. **AI may polish those words, but it does not decide the message.**

If AI writes first, its draft starts from what feedback like this usually says, and that can shape the message before your own facts are down.

> 💡 If the words carry a consequence for someone, own the first draft and the last read.
