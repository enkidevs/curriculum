# Safeguards By Task

---
## Content

**Match the safeguard to the way the task can fail.**

Figures fail quietly. A plausible number is simply wrong, so recalculate it with a tested method and compare it with the source.

Customer or public copy fails by inventing: a promise, a timeline, a comparison. Verify every claim against approved facts before it goes out.

Work about a named person fails when generic patterns stand in for judgment. You supply the facts and the judgment; AI only polishes the wording, in an approved tool.

The rule is simple: name the likely failure, then choose the check that catches it.

> 💡 A vague "double-check it" catches nothing. Decide what wrong looks like here, then look for exactly that.
