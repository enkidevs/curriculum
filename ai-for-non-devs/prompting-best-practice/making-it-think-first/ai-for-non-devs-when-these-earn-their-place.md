# When Extra Steps Help

---
## Content

Guided steps and step-back prompting both add more to write and read.

**Use them when you need to check or explain the reasoning**:

- comparing options or tradeoffs
- making a judgment you must explain
- drafting a policy
- handling a mistake that would be costly

Skip them for a quick rewrite, a two-line reply, or a rough first draft.

> 💡 Use the extra steps when you need to check the logic, assumptions, or tradeoffs. Skip them when the task is simple and low-risk.
