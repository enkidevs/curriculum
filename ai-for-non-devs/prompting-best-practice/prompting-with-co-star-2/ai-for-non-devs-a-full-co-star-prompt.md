# A Full CO-STAR Prompt

---
## Content

Here's a prompt with all six parts. Say you lead a support team, and a new 48-hour response target starts Monday:

```plain-text
## Context
We're a 12-person support team. Monday's response target drops from 72 hours to 48. Staffing stays the same.

## Objective
Write the briefing note for our team channel.

## Style
Short sentences, headings, no preamble. Use bullets for lists.

## Tone
Direct and calm. Acknowledge the pressure without apologizing.

## Audience
Support staff who know the queue and tools, but not why the target changed.

## Response
Under 200 words. End with "What to do if you can't hit it".
```

**It's just everything the assistant would otherwise have guessed.**

> 💡 Before you send an important prompt, check that it has a line for each of the six.
