# Bullets, Tables Or Prose

---
## Content

**Once you ask for a shape, match it to the job.**

**Bullets** suit a list someone will scan, like the five checks a duty manager runs before opening, one line each on a card.

**A table** suits a comparison, with each office option on a row and each difference in a column:

| Option | Cost per year | Notice period |
|---|---|---|
| Stay put | $18,000 | none |
| Move to Riverside | $14,400 | 3 months |
| Hybrid desks | $9,600 | 1 month |

**Prose** suits reasoning. If your team needs to follow why a shift plan changed, full sentences keep each reason tied to the decision.

> ‼️ Bullets make scanning easy, but they can turn an argument into disconnected claims.
