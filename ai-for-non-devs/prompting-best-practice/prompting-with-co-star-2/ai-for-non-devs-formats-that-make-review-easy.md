# Formats That Make Review Easy

---
## Content

**The format that reads best is not always the format that's easiest to check.**

Put the claim and its evidence side by side:

```plain-text
Summarize what parents asked at Tuesday's open evening from these feedback forms. Use a two-column table: question raised | words from the form that support it. One point per row.
```

Now you can check each summary point against the words beside it.

> 💡 Get the checkable shape first. Smooth prose can come after the check.

---
## Practice

You'll compare every summary point with the source before sending. What output shape makes that fastest?

???

- A two-column table with each point beside its source wording.

---
## Revision

Why is a smooth summary that blends several sources into each sentence difficult to verify?

???

- No claim can be traced back without rereading everything.
