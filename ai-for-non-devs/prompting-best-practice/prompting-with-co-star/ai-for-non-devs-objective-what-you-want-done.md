# Objective: What You Want Done

---
## Content

**O is for Objective.** Ask yourself: what do I want AI to produce?

**Start with an action verb.**

**🚫 Subject**

```plain-text
Help me with the delivery problem we're having with our packaging supplier.
```

**✅ Task**

```plain-text
Draft a five-line email asking our packaging supplier to confirm a new delivery date and explain what they'll do if it slips again.
```

"Supplier problem" names a subject. "Draft an email" names the task and the deliverable.

> 💡 A subject invites an overview. A verb asks for something you can use.
