# Keeping Saved Prompts Current

---
## Content

A saved prompt can stay correct while the work around it changes.

Your digest prompt groups complaints by site. It was designed when there were four sites.

There are now 11, and the accurate output is too long to scan on a phone during the meeting.

Nothing is factually wrong. The format has stopped serving its reader.

**Review a saved prompt when its audience, input, workflow, tool, or required output changes.** Also review it when you keep making the same new edit.

> 💡 When you reuse a saved prompt, check it against today's job: who reads it, how much goes in, and where they read it.
