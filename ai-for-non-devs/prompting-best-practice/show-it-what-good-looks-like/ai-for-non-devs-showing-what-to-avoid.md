# Counter-Examples

---
## Content

A counter-example helps when the same specific failure keeps returning. Your thank-you notes to fundraiser donors keep ending with a nudge toward the next gift.

"Do not ask for more money" has not fixed it. Show the contrast instead:

**🚫 Weak**

```plain-text
Thank you for your gift. With your continued support, we can print even more workbooks next year.
```

**✅ Stronger**

```plain-text
Thank you for your gift. It covered the printing for all 400 workbooks we handed out in March.
```

Both notes have the same job and structure. Only the second sentence changes: one hints at another gift, while the other names what the money paid for and stops.

**A counter-example shows the unwanted pattern beside the target**, which a "don't" on its own can't do.

> 💡 Show the mistake next to the correct version, so AI can see the difference you mean.
