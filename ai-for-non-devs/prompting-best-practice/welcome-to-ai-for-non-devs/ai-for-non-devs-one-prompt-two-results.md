# One Prompt, Two Results

---
## Content

Two attempts at the same email, by the same person, two minutes apart.

Compare what went in:

**🚫 Weak**

```plain-text
Write an email to a customer about a delayed order.
```

**✅ Stronger**

```plain-text
Write an email to a customer whose order shipped four days late after a warehouse system outage. She has emailed us twice. Apologize once, say what happened, give the new delivery date, and offer free return shipping. Under 120 words.
```

The first comes back as a template with blanks to fill in.

The second comes back as something you could send after one read.

**Nothing about the AI changed between those two attempts.** Everything about the input did.

> 💡 The quality of what comes back is set before you press enter.
