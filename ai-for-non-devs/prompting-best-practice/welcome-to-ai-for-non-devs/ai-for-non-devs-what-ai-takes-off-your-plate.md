# What AI Takes Off Your Plate

---
## Content

AI earns its keep on the work that swallows an hour and produces something you were going to edit anyway.

- **Drafting.** A first version of an email, an agenda, a job ad.
- **Summarizing.** Twelve pages down to the six lines your manager needs.
- **Comparing.** Two proposals set against criteria you name.
- **Organizing.** Ninety open-ended survey answers sorted into themes.
- **Reshaping.** The same update rewritten for a different reader.


**Notice what's missing from that list: deciding.**

It can sort those ninety answers quickly, but it's not going to be the final decision-maker for what your team should act on.

> 💡 Let AI do the producing. Keep the deciding for yourself.
