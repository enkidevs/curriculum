# What You'll Learn

---
## Content

AI can save you time on work you already do: writing, summarizing, planning, researching, and generating ideas.

But getting useful results takes more than typing a question and hoping for the best.

> 💡 You'll learn how to write better prompts, recognize where AI is useful, and know when its answers need checking.

Later, we'll compare different AI tools and look at how they fit different kinds of work.

**The goal isn't just to use AI. It's to know how to use it well.**
