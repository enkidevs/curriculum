# What's Ahead

---
## Content

The rest of this subtopic is the how:

- **How AI actually works.** What it's doing when it answers you. The techniques make more sense once you know why they work.
- **CO-STAR.** One framework covering the six things a prompt usually needs.
- **The techniques.** Showing it an example, asking for what you want instead of what you don't, making it reason before it answers, and turning a mediocre first answer into a good one.
- **Judgment.** Mostly knowing when to close the chat and do it yourself.
