# Where It Shines And Where It Doesn't

---
## Content

**The shape of a task tells you more about how AI will do than the subject does.**

It works well when many answers could be acceptable and producing any of them takes time:

- Twelve ways to thank a room of volunteers
- An outline for a workshop
- A clumsy paragraph, rewritten

Be careful where exactly one answer is right and that answer is already recorded somewhere:

- Which version of the supplier agreement got signed
- What the travel policy actually allows
- Who approved the extra spend, and when

Those you check against the source.

> 💡 Many possible answers? Let AI draft. One correct answer? Check the source.
